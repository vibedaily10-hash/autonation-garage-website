Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the information you share when you contact AutoNation, request a service booking, ask about spare parts, or communicate with us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or website forms. This may include your name, contact details, vehicle details, service needs, and order enquiries.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, confirm bookings, provide vehicle care services, source parts, manage customer communication, improve our website, and maintain service records.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly website analytics to understand page visits and improve the site. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not intended to identify individual visitors.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details with trusted service providers only when needed to complete a booking, process communication, provide delivery, or comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information where applicable. You can also choose not to provide optional information, although this may limit our ability to complete a service or parts request.
